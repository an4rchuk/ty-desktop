    When you sign in
    Game mode

    When a game's in front for a few seconds (anything from Steam, Epic, Riot, Xbox, Roblox, Minecraft…, or any fullscreen app that isn't a browser), her big model leaves your GPU and a tiny Qwen3 1.7B answers from the CPU instead: 0 VRAM. Plain commands (open, close, music, mute, timers, “move spotify to my samsung”) skip the AI completely and happen instantly. Back to normal 40 seconds after you leave the game. Only on Auto.

    Moving windows

    “move spotify to my samsung”, “put discord on monitor 2”, “chuck chrome on the other screen”, “send it to the main monitor”. Monitor 1 is your main one. Keeps its place and its share of the screen, maximised again if it was.

    Watching your screen

    Every few minutes she looks at your screen and says one thing about it: a roast, a hype, a tip. Her eyes are a tiny 2B vision model, and nothing is saved. Also: “watch my screen” / “stop watching my screen”.

    Reminders and timers

    “remind me in 20 min to take the pizza out”, “set a timer for 5 minutes”, “remind me at 6pm to call mum”, “what are my reminders”, “cancel my reminders”. A Windows notification and her popping up. Ctrl+Alt+T opens her chat window from anywhere.

    Discord (“mute me”, “deafen me”)

    Out of the box Ty presses Discord's own Ctrl+Shift+M / Ctrl+Shift+D by bringing Discord to the front for a split second, which can't reach Discord while it's only in the tray. For a clean toggle that works anywhere: in Discord › User Settings › Keybinds › Add a Keybind › Toggle Mute (and Toggle Deafen), record something like Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M, and type the same here.

    PC power and windows

    “Shut down”, “restart” and “sleep” always get a button first: nothing happens until you click it. “Lock my PC”, “minimise everything” and “bring them back” just happen.

    What she can and can't touch

    Files: reads and saves in your user folders and other drives. Never Windows, System32, Program Files, ProgramData, AppData, or anything with passwords or keys. Apps: types and presses keys only after your click, never in terminals, PowerShell, the Registry Editor or Task Manager. Web: only when you ask her to search.
